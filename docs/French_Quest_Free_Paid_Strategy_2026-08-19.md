# French Quest — Free / Paid Conversion Strategy

Date: 2026-08-19

## Core principle

French Quest should not make the free version intentionally weak.

The product logic should be:

- Free = practice + basic feedback
- Paid = diagnosis + direction + progression

A simple framing:

- XP answers: 「你練了多少」
- Skill performance answers: 「你做得多好」
- Roadmap answers: 「你下一步去哪裡」

XP should support engagement and retention, not represent language level.

## Free user experience

Free users should receive enough value to understand their current performance, for example:

- Mission score
- Basic skill performance
- Weak spot count
- Strongest area
- One clear “focus next” suggestion
- Total XP / activity reward
- Basic Review Mode

Example:

Reading 64%  
Situation & Response 82%  
Vocabulary 88%  
Weak spots: 5

Strongest area: Vocabulary  
Focus next: Reading

The free experience should let the learner discover that there is a real pattern in their strengths and weaknesses.

## Paid user experience

Paid users unlock deeper interpretation and a concrete learning path, including:

- Full A2 → B1 readiness roadmap
- Detailed weak-point analysis
- Performance trends across attempts / missions
- Common mistake patterns
- Personalized next mission / next practice recommendation
- Readiness indicators and missing criteria
- Progress over time
- More complete content library

Example:

A2 Reading — Building

Common weak areas:
- time/date information
- restriction words such as seulement
- inferring the next action from a status message

Recommended next steps:
1. Clear 3 reading weak spots
2. Complete Grocery Store reading set
3. Reach stable performance across multiple missions

## Conversion principle

The strongest upgrade moment is after the learner has generated meaningful personal data.

Best conversion moments:

1. Mission Complete
2. Review Complete
3. Roadmap preview / locked readiness milestone

Example message:

“You scored 19/25.
Vocabulary is strong, but Reading is less consistent.

Want to know what’s holding back your A2 progress?
Unlock your full readiness roadmap.”

## Paywall strategy

Do NOT primarily paywall:

- basic mission scores
- basic wrong-answer review
- basic TEF Tips
- basic weak-spot visibility

Better paid value:

- analysis
- historical trends
- roadmap
- personalized recommendations
- deeper diagnosis
- broader mission/content access

## Roadmap teaser concept

Free users may see a partial roadmap:

A1 Foundation ✅  
A2 Building 🔓  
A2 Ready 🔒  
B1 Building 🔒

Possible teaser:

“2 of 4 readiness indicators available.
Unlock the full roadmap to see what you need next.”

## Important product distinction

Do not equate XP with TEF level.

Do not claim that a certain XP number or raw score guarantees A2/B1.

Readiness should eventually be based on calibrated performance across multiple skills, missions, and attempts.

## Monetization thesis

French Quest’s paid value should answer three questions:

1. Where am I now?
2. Why am I stuck?
3. What should I do next?

Roadmap + personalized diagnosis is the monetization engine.
XP is mainly a retention / engagement layer.
